ARTIST STUDIO / USER GUIDE

Lyrics & video studio

Correct existing subtitles against the music, then turn a selected section into a social video with burned-in animated lyrics.

Start with a track

  1. Connect to Studio and open Lyrics & video studio in the sidebar.
  2. Choose a track. The list includes saved subtitle sidecars and tracks with supplied lyric lines.
  3. Studio loads the track’s saved manual corrections, or its automatic analysis if no corrections exist, plus its artwork and audio.
  4. Press Play. Click a line to seek to it, then enable Loop line to hear it repeatedly.

Try the interactive four-second demo without a token. It uses a generated tone and makes no repository changes.

A compact video-editor workspace

The editor fits the viewport. The transcript is on the left, the video preview in the centre, and the inspector on the right. The waveform and transport stay together in a fixed bottom pane. Each content pane scrolls independently; you do not need to scroll the page to get back to Play.

Drag the separators to resize the transcript, inspector or timeline. Transcript/Inspector buttons can hide side panes. On narrow screens, Preview/Transcript/Inspector tabs switch the upper pane while the waveform stays visible. Layout sizes are remembered on this device.

The inspector has Video, Timing and Assets tabs. Timing holds the optional numeric line/word form; Assets holds artwork upload, local audio and SRT/VTT/project downloads. Clip in/out controls and common line actions sit beside the waveform. Export stays docked at the bottom of the inspector.

Edit the waveform timeline

In Lines mode, each region represents a subtitle line. Double-click its text label to edit the subtitle directly on the waveform. Enter finishes the edit; Esc restores its original text. In Words mode, labels edit individual words. Drag the region body away from the label to move timing, or drag its edges to resize. The numeric Timing inspector remains available as a secondary editing method. Drag a region to move it, or drag its edges to change the start/end. Its existing word times move and scale with it. Zoom in to make small changes easier. In Words mode, only the selected line’s timed words are shown; drag their edges for precise word highlighting.

You can also enter exact seconds in the line and word fields. Use Start/End = playhead after positioning the cursor by listening. Timing is checked before Save/Publish and export. Lines must stay within the track, and timed words must stay within their line.

Understand word timing

Source timing came from the supplied/recognised track analysis. Manual means you changed a word’s timing. Estimated means positions are approximate. Untimed words have no start/end.

Changing the number of words clears their old word positions because the alignment is no longer valid. Choose Estimate word positions to distribute words approximately within the line, then listen and correct them. This is an editing aid, not speech recognition. It does not silently claim an estimated timestamp was recognised. Line-only fade/glow styles can still be useful before every word is timed.

Keyboard shortcuts

When focus is not in an input: Space plays/pauses, left/right seeks 0.1 seconds, Shift+left/right nudges the selected line 0.01 seconds, and I/O sets its start/end from the playhead. Normal typing and browser shortcuts remain available inside fields.

Design the video

Choose a ratio using the rectangle icons: 1:1 square, 3:4 portrait, 4:5 portrait, 16:9 landscape, or 9:16 vertical. At full quality these are 1080×1080, 1080×1440, 1080×1350, 1920×1080 and 1080×1920. The 720 option scales them down for a quicker, smaller export. Output is 30 fps.

Set Clip in/out in seconds, use the playhead buttons, or select Use selected line as clip. The default is the first 30 seconds (or the complete track if shorter). Clips can be up to 10 minutes, subject to browser memory. Choose shorter clips for social posts and first exports.

Choose Karaoke highlight, Kinetic word pop, Neon glow, Clean fade or Typewriter reveal. Karaoke/pop use available word times; untimed words fall back to the line rather than pretending to have exact alignment. Adjust typography, text and highlight colours, size, vertical position, background darkening and crop focus. Toggle a slow artwork zoom, track/artist title, progress line or uppercase text. Safe-area guides are visible only in the editor.

The canvas preview and export use the same renderer. The preview itself is reduced in resolution for responsiveness; the exported video uses the selected output dimensions.

Track artwork or upload

The saved track artwork is the default background. Upload a PNG, JPEG or WebP to replace it. The editor scales an upload to a maximum edge of 1920 and compresses it for the saved project; images above 25 MB or still above the project limit after compression are rejected. Use Crop focus X/Y to keep the important part visible for each ratio.

Changing ratios does not create a separate stored preset for each ratio. Check the crop and text after switching. Use track artwork restores the default. Uploaded artwork stays local until you save the video project.

Export an actual MP4

  1. Check the clip, artwork, timing and output-size estimate.
  2. Press Export MP4.
  3. Studio checks for H.264 video and AAC audio encoding. Where supported, it renders frames directly in the browser, independently of live playback.
  4. If H.264/AAC encoding is unavailable, the MP4 button stays disabled and the editor explains the browser requirement. Timing edits, subtitles and project downloads still work.
  5. Watch the progress indicator or cancel. The resulting MP4 downloads with the selected audio section and burned-in lyrics.

No microphone, screen recording, backend or site publication is required. Audio channels are preserved up to stereo. The exported clip starts at time zero; the renderer uses the original track timestamps internally to keep the lyrics aligned.

MP4 availability depends on the browser’s codecs. The editor does not offer real-time screen/canvas recording as a substitute, because dropped initial frames would undermine lyric synchronisation. Use the HTTPS Studio address in current Chrome/Edge or Safari. If encoding is unavailable, Studio explains that and does not rename a WebM file as MP4. HTTP and HTTPS use separate browser storage, so you may need to reconnect once after switching. Large exports use browser memory; start with a short 720 clip. Keep the tab open until download completes.

Audio or artwork cannot load

Streaming playback does not guarantee the media host allows downloading/decoding for a waveform or canvas export. If a host blocks cross-origin access, use Local audio fallback to choose the matching audio file, or upload the artwork. Local audio is session-only and is never uploaded or committed. A duration mismatch prevents export so you do not unknowingly render the wrong audio. The controls still let you edit text/timestamps when remote audio is unavailable.

Save corrections safely

The main Save draft / Publish toolbar includes the timing corrections and video settings you changed. Timing edits go to lyric-edits/TRACK_ID.json; design settings go to video-projects/TRACK_ID.json. Manual timing files are separate from automatic lyrics/ results. During the public build, validated manual corrections are applied after automatic results and before approximate gap filling. Analysis does not overwrite the corrections.

Save draft uses the existing draft branch. Publish makes the corrected subtitles available in the website’s lyric player after deployment. Only edits made in this session are added to the save payload; simply opening a track does not overwrite its data. JSON project downloads and local recovery do not contain a GitHub token. Local drafts can become large when artwork is included; heed storage warnings and keep an exported backup.

Download SRT/VTT exports the full-track subtitle timings. Download project JSON exports the selected track metadata, current timing document and design options. There is no project/SRT import control in this version. The editor does not regenerate speech recognition, sync external video services or publish videos to social networks.

Practice a region and recover original sentences

Select a subtitle and click Loop selected to repeat it with a short lead-in and tail. Choose None, 0.5, 1 or 2 seconds of context. Drag the amber loop strip or enter A/B times to practise a custom range. A/B can also be set to the playhead. This loop is independent of the export clip.

Replay (R) restarts at A; Loop region (L) toggles repeating. + Sentence opens the insertion composer. Choose before/after the selected sentence, a playhead gap, or the current loop. Paste one or several sentences, review suggested timings, then insert and listen. Estimated word positions are a starting point for manual refinement.

The transcript pane has Edited and Original tabs. Original shows the track’s original lyrics, including sentences recognition missed. Copy a sentence or the full transcript, use a sentence to replace the selected subtitle while keeping its timing, or press + to insert it in the loop. These changes can be undone; the original transcript stays unchanged.

Correcting words without losing timing

Changing a sentence keeps timestamps for words that still match, including punctuation and case changes. Added or replaced words are estimated inside the surrounding timing gap. If there is no gap, only adjacent word spans are redistributed; the rest of the sentence stays anchored. Approximate syllable count and word length guide distribution. These adjusted words are labelled Estimated: replay the area and refine their edges when needed. Deleting words preserves the remaining matched words. Undo restores the previous sentence and timing.

Lines, Words and Sentence views

Lines zooms out to the full track for arranging subtitles. Double-click a line region or a row in Edited to open it in Sentence mode. Words shows draggable words across sentence boundaries, zoomed to a four-second window around the playhead. Scroll horizontally to edit neighbouring sentences without entering a practice loop. Sentence fits just the sentence and lead/tail, with a focused playback loop. Only one view is active at a time. Returning to Lines or Words restores your earlier loop settings and fits the appropriate view. Use the zoom slider for manual adjustments or click the active view again to refit.

Sentence mode: visual word timing

Select a sentence in Edited, then click Sentence above the waveform. The view automatically zooms to that sentence with lead/tail context and starts looping. The sentence has its own boundary lane above separate word regions in alternating lanes. Move or resize the sentence lane to carry its words with it. Word chips above the waveform seek to individual words. Double-click a chip to zoom to that word, then use Fit sentence to return. Drag a word body to move it; drag its left or right edge to change its start or end. Larger handles make fine timing easier. Playback keeps cycling while you adjust.

Prev / Next moves through sentences and fits the next sentence automatically. Fit sentence restores the fit after manual zooming. Words remain individually editable by double-clicking their label. Click Lines or Words to leave Sentence mode. Changes are saved with the usual Save draft / Publish controls.

Audio visualizers

Open Visuals in the inspector. Choose Spectrum bars, Mirrored bars, Radial spectrum, Pulse ring, Waveform or Frequency dots. None keeps the original artwork-and-lyrics layout. Each style remembers its own position, scale, rotation, opacity, colours and response settings.

Drag the visualizer in the preview to place it. Drag the white corner to scale it. The X/Y and Scale sliders provide another way to adjust placement. Centre and Lower third offer quick positioning. With the preview focused, arrow keys move the visualizer, and Shift moves it further. Position and size adapt to the chosen aspect ratio. Large visualizers can extend beyond the canvas and crop intentionally.

Choose a primary/accent colour, a palette, glow and line weight. Sensitivity changes the amount of movement. Smoothing softens changes over time. Detail controls the number of frequency bands. Waveform uses the audio signal directly, so frequency detail and smoothing do not apply. Bars and dots determine their own width from the band count. Choose Behind lyrics or In front of lyrics. Reset style restores only the current style.

The visualizer responds to the loaded track audio, with identical timestamp-based analysis in preview and MP4 export. Preview playback is needed to watch it move, but exported frames render offline. No microphone access is needed. Without decoded audio, you see a resting shape. Selection guides never appear in the export. Save draft / Publish stores visualizer settings with the video project. Older projects keep their existing appearance until you choose a visualizer.

Insert, recover and delete sentences

Each Edited transcript row has + Before, + After and Delete. The transcript’s Insert / paste sentences and timeline’s + Sentence open the same composer. The playhead insertion button starts in At playhead mode. Paste one line per sentence. Section headings such as [Verse] are skipped. You can insert up to 100 sentences at once.

The composer previews every proposed time range. Matching original alignment timings inside the chosen gap take priority. Other lines receive estimated sentence and word timings within the free space. Audio activity can trim quiet edges, but this does not recognise words or perform speech-based forced alignment. Suggested words remain labelled Estimated. Long empty outros do not stretch a short new sentence across the entire remainder of the song.

When there is not enough space, the composer asks you to choose another gap or use the loop range. Inserting into a loop can overlap existing lyrics only when you explicitly enable the overlap option. No existing sentence is shifted or shortened. Listen to suggestion previews the range before inserting. Cancel restores the previous playback position and loop.

Insert & review creates the sentences together as one undoable change and opens Sentence mode on the first addition. Listen, drag the sentence boundary or word regions, and use Prev / Next to review the batch. Needs review marks estimated insertions. Mark reviewed acknowledges your check while estimated word provenance remains visible. Save draft / Publish stores the changes with the usual manual lyric edits.

In Original, select one or more missing sentences and choose Insert selected before/after. A single source row also has Insert before/after buttons. Replace keeps the selected sentence’s time range and preserves matching word anchors. Delete removes only the chosen subtitle, leaving later timestamps and original source lyrics untouched. Undo restores insertion, deletion or review changes.
